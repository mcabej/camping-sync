# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Friends organising a shared camping trip, often from their phones and sometimes
with poor connectivity. They need to decide what is happening, divide practical
responsibilities, and recover those decisions later without searching across a
separate group chat.

## Product Purpose

Camping Sync gives a group one durable place for the trip: what to bring, eat,
and do; who owns each responsibility; what is packed; and the planning
conversation around those decisions. Success means the group can see what is
covered, what still needs attention, and why a decision was made.

## Brand Personality

Practical, calm, outdoorsy. The product should feel like dependable trail
wayfinding: distinctive enough to orient people, quiet enough to keep the task
in front.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic workplace dashboard or a noisy Messenger/Slack-style
chat client. Avoid decorative gamification, novelty controls, and visual effects
that compete with trip status.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the whole group oriented at a glance.
2. Put decisions beside the trip they affect.
3. Make ownership and unresolved work unmistakable.
4. Prefer familiar, low-friction controls over clever interaction.
5. Keep essential state useful on a phone and resilient around poor signal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA as a practical baseline: keyboard-operable controls, visible
focus, semantic labels, adequate contrast, color-independent meaning, sensible
touch targets, and reduced-motion support. Meet these needs within the existing
visual character rather than stripping it away.
